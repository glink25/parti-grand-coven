How to Play

Each round, secretly choose four of the eight roles. The lead player starts one of their remaining roles and chooses Favor for a safe immediate effect or Claim for the stronger action.

Going clockwise, every player who also chose that role must respond. A later Claim steals the main action. When the circle ends, only the final claimant gets the Claim effect.

After five rounds, score potion and ritual VP plus 1 VP per 3 coins. Ties go to the player with more herb + mana + crystal, then remain shared.

  • 💎 Miner — crystals
